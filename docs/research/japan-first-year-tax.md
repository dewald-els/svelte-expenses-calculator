# Japan first-year tax notes (foreign worker)

Primary-source summary used for the gross-to-net estimator in this app.

## What can be lower in year 1?

- Inhabitant tax (resident tax) is generally based on prior-year income and Jan 1 status, so newcomers often have lower/zero inhabitant-tax withholding in their first year, then higher deductions later.  
  Source: https://www.tax.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/kazei/life/kojin_ju

## Residency matters

- NTA distinguishes resident vs non-resident taxation. Non-resident treatment can differ substantially, including separate withholding treatment in many cases.  
  Source: https://www.nta.go.jp/english/taxes/individual/12006.htm

- Employees’ Pension Insurance contribution handling (employee/employer share).  
  Source: https://www.nenkin.go.jp/international/english/japanese-system/employeespension/epi_contribution.html
- Enrollment/procedure context for employed workers.  
  Source: https://www.nenkin.go.jp/international/english/japanese-system/employeespension/epi_procedure.html

## App caveat

This planner uses a simplified estimate. Actual withholding and annual settlement can differ by municipality, payroll setup, deductions, dependents, and treaty status. Verify with official payroll statements and tax notices.
